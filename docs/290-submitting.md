# Submitting your project

## What to include

- a link to your finished project, live if possible, not just a repo
- a short writeup of what it does
- what you learned or what was hard about building it
- screenshots or a short demo video if it's not obvious from just visiting the link

## What reviewers are actually checking for

Whether the project is real and working, whether the time logged in Hackatime roughly matches what you built, and whether there's something in it that's clearly yours and not lifted straight from a tutorial.

## Common reasons submissions get sent back

- the project doesn't actually run or the link is broken
- barely any Hackatime data logged
- it's a nearly exact copy of a tutorial with nothing changed

Fix whatever's flagged and resubmit, it's not a one shot thing.
